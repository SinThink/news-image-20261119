19 NOVEMBER 2026
APEC · Shenzhen
TRUMP, XI AND PUTIN HOLD FIRST THREE-WAY SUMMIT
The three leaders met for 90 minutes in Shenzhen and issued a four-point “peace declaration”: UN reform, more refined-fuel supply, and the pledge that “a nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought,” plus talks on nuclear risk. It said nothing about Ukraine or Iran and set no timetable.
FIRST-EVER MEETING OF THE THREE · APEC, SHENZHEN
World Court
WORLD COURT: MYANMAR RESPONSIBLE OVER ROHINGYA
Landmark ICJ ruling — the first time a state is held responsible for the 2017 campaign against the Rohingya. Thousands were killed; sexual violence was widespread. Brought by Gambia; final, with no appeal.
Ukraine
UKRAINE HITS KERCH BRIDGE A THIRD TIME IN FOUR DAYS
A strike on the railway section halted train traffic; no casualties were reported. The road span had already collapsed on 17 November.
Riyadh
ATTACK DAMAGES PIF TOWER IN RIYADH
Saudi Arabia’s tallest skyscraper and home of its sovereign wealth fund: windows blown out across 7–8 floors, the facade bent, no injuries. The second strike on the financial district in under two weeks, after the Aramco building on 8 Nov.
Bangladesh
FOUR DEAD AS UNREST SWEEPS BANGLADESH
An Awami League lockdown, blasts in Dhaka and violence in 28 of 64 districts. The family home of Muhammad Yunus was set on fire and Grameen Bank branches were attacked.
Markets
ANTHROPIC IPO BREAKS RECORD AT $81.3 BILLION
Priced at $83 for 980 million shares and listed on Nasdaq as ANT on 19 Nov, valuing it near $1.5 trillion. It opened at $111.50 and closed at $97.17.
